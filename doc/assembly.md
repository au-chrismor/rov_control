# Assembly

1.  Flash current release of rov_control software onto the controller and verify operation

1.  After testing is completed, disconnect the gear motors, test power and test RS485 harness from the electonics pod.

1.  Remove the sled from the electonics pod.

1.  Install the expansion tube and fitting to the pod shell.

1.  Glue the cable end cap to the pod body.  Allow to dry (2 Hours).

1.  Wash the electonics pod, expansion tube and end-caps with isopropyl alcohol ("IPA") and allow to dry.

1.  Wash the sled with IPA and allow to dry.

1.  Dry all components at 30C for 24 hours.

1.  Reassemble the pod in a clean environment.  Ensure all connections are secure.

1.  Run a diagnostic on all components and verify correct operation.

1.  With the expansion tube open and held above the top of the pod body, fill the body with **filtered** mineral oil until approximately 10mm below the top edge of the pod.

1.  Run a final diagnostic check on the electronics.

1.  Wipe the top lip of the electonics pod with IPA to clean

1.  Prime and glue the end cap in place.  Wait 2 hours to bond.

1.  Check for leaks and correct as appropriate.

1.  Attach a funnel to the end of the expansion tube and fill with **filtered** mineral oil.

1.  Fold and clamp the expansion tube.
