# Service Procedures

## Post-dive

1.  Place ROV in **fresh water** tank and run all motors for 5 minutes to flush out.

1.  Dry the electonics pod and check for physical damage or leaks.

1.  Check motors and propellors for physical damage.

1.  Check frame for physical damage.

## Six Months of Operation

1.  Complete Post-dive steps.

1.  Drain oil via expansion tube.  Make sure that all oil is captured in a clean container.

1.  Check oil for signs of contamination or water.

1.  Refill with **clean**, **filtered** mineral oil.

1.  Load-test main battery pack.

1.  Re-assemble and tank-test.
